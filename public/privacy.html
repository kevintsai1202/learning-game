知識島大冒險 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

English version below

「知識島大冒險」是給國小二年級孩子的 3D 學習遊戲（網址 https://kevintsai1202.github.io/learning-game/，備用網址 https://learning-island.zeabur.app/）。本政策說明我們收集哪些資料、為什麼收集、存在哪裡、保存多久、誰看得到，以及使用 Google 帳號登入時取得的資料如何使用。我們只收集提供遊戲功能需要的資料；不放廣告、不出售或分享資料，也不使用任何追蹤或分析工具。

1. 適用範圍與營運者

本政策適用於「知識島大冒險」的網站、遊戲與班級伺服器。本服務由個人開發者（GitHub 帳號 kevintsai1202）開發與維運，以下稱「我們」。遊戲免費，不需要註冊也可以單機遊玩；家長與老師的帳號、加入班級、Google 帳號登入都是選用功能。帳號是給家長與老師使用的；孩子不需要註冊：由家長掃老師的 QR code 讓孩子加入班級，或在學校的平板上由老師輸入教室密碼讓孩子進入。

3. 資料的用途

  • 保存與同步學習進度，讓孩子換裝置也能接著玩；家長可以用自己的帳號把孩子的角色存到雲端，在新裝置找回。
  • 提供班級功能：多人同島、短句聊天、送禮物。
  • 讓老師管理班級、查看學習摘要。
  • 登入與帳號安全：辨識登入的人，防止猜密碼與大量請求。
  • 電子郵件地址只用來寄送驗證信與重設密碼的信（見第 6 節），不寄廣告或電子報。

我們不會把資料用於廣告、行銷、使用者分析或剖析，不會出售資料，也不會用來訓練人工智慧模型。

4. Google 使用者資料（使用 Google 帳號登入）

家長或老師可以選擇用 Google 帳號註冊，或在帳號設定把 Google 帳號綁定到自己的帳號，之後在帳號頁按 Google 的「透過 Google 帳戶繼續操作」按鈕快速登入。這是選用功能：用帳號名稱與密碼註冊的帳號，一直可以用帳號名稱與密碼登入；用 Google 註冊的帳號不用另外設定帳號名稱與密碼，之後可以在帳號設定加上密碼。孩子的角色不會綁定 Google 帳號。

  • 要求的權限：只有 openid、email、profile（Google 歸類為非敏感範圍）。我們不會存取 Gmail 信箱、Google 雲端硬碟、聯絡人、日曆或任何其他 Google 服務的資料。
  • 取得的資料：登入時，Google 提供的登入按鈕（Google Identity Services）會交給我們一張由 Google 簽署的登入憑證（ID token），內含 Google 帳號識別碼、電子郵件地址（以及 Google 是否驗證過這個地址）、名稱與大頭貼網址。伺服器用 Google 公開的金鑰驗證憑證確實來自 Google。
  • 如何使用：Google 帳號識別碼只用來找出綁定的家長或老師帳號，完成登入，以及在設定密碼或刪除帳號時確認是本人。用 Google 註冊時，Google 驗證過的電子郵件地址會成為帳號的電子郵件地址（用途見第 3 節），帳號名稱由這個電子郵件地址「@」前面的英文字母、數字與底線自動產生（只有你自己在帳號頁看得到）；綁定時記下的電子郵件地址只用來在帳號設定顯示「綁定的是哪個 Google 帳號」，畫面上會遮住部分字元。
  • 保存的資料：只保存 Google 帳號識別碼、電子郵件地址與綁定時間，存在班級伺服器的資料庫。名稱、大頭貼與憑證裡的其他資料一律不保存。
  • 分享與揭露：Google 使用者資料不會出售、不會用於廣告、不會提供或轉移給任何第三方，也不會用來訓練人工智慧模型。除了維護系統安全或依法律要求之外，不會有人檢視這些資料。老師看不到家長的帳號資料與電子郵件地址。
  • 刪除與撤銷：隨時可以在帳號設定按「解除」，伺服器會立即刪除該筆綁定（Google 帳號識別碼與電子郵件地址；沒有密碼的帳號要先設定密碼，才能解除最後一個 Google 帳號，以免沒有辦法登入）；刪除帳號時，所有綁定一併刪除。也可以到 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式和服務」頁面（https://myaccount.google.com/connections）移除「知識島大冒險」的登入權限。
  • 有限使用聲明：知識島大冒險對於從 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與轉移，將遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策（Google API Services User Data Policy），包括其中的有限使用（Limited Use）規定。

5. 誰看得到這些資料

  • 同一班的同學：在這一班的暱稱、角色外觀、是否上線、目前在哪座島（只寫「在班級島」或「在自己的島」，不顯示是哪一班，顯示在好友名單上）、在這一班的班級島上的位置、聊天短句（遊戲裡沒有可以自由打字的地方）；選禮物時看得到對方已經有哪些外觀（避免送重複）；收到的禮物會顯示送禮人在那一班的暱稱。孩子在別的班級用的暱稱與班級名稱，這一班的同學看不到。
  • 兄弟姊妹（同一個家長帳號名下的雲端角色）：好友名單上的暱稱、角色外觀、是否上線、目前在哪座島（只寫「在班級島」或「在自己的島」，不顯示班級名稱），不同班也看得到。
  • 老師：班上每個孩子在這一班的暱稱、星星、金幣、錯題數、完成回合數（學習進度是孩子整個角色的，包括在其他班級島與自己的島上玩的）、加入這一班的時間、最後上線時間，以及現在在哪裡（是否上線、在這一班的班級島、別的班級島（不顯示是哪一班）或自己的島、在哪棟建築）。老師可以重設孩子在這一班的密碼、把孩子移出這一班，以及開關加入、聊天與送禮功能。老師看不到孩子還在哪些其他班級，也看不到家長的帳號資料與電子郵件地址。
  • 家長：用家長帳號登入後，看得到自己名下雲端角色的摘要（名字、外觀、所在班級、星星、金幣、最後上線時間），可以把角色下載到這台裝置、讓角色退出班級或刪除角色；裝置上的家長專區看得到這台裝置上角色的完整進度與錯題本。
  • 帳號本人：帳號設定看得到自己的帳號名稱、電子郵件地址、身分與綁定的 Google 帳號。
  • 班級以外的人看不到班級裡的任何資料（兄弟姊妹只看得到上面列的好友名單資料）。

6. 第三方服務與資料存放位置

  • GitHub Pages（GitHub, Inc.）：提供網站檔案。GitHub 可能依 GitHub 隱私權聲明記錄連線資料（例如 IP 位址）。
  • Zeabur：用來部署與管理班級伺服器和資料庫。伺服器與資料庫位於台灣；網際網路連線經由位於日本東京的閘道伺服器轉送。
  • Google（登入）：只有畫面出現「使用 Google 帳戶登入」按鈕時，才會載入 Google 的登入程式；使用時適用 Google 隱私權政策。
  • Google（寄信）：驗證信與重設密碼的信透過 Google 的 Gmail 寄送，所以收件人的電子郵件地址與信件內容（帳號名稱與連結）會經過 Google 的郵件伺服器，適用 Google 隱私權政策。寄件人是我們的 Gmail 帳號。
  • 除此之外，不使用廣告、分析、追蹤或其他第三方服務。我們不會把資料提供給第三方，法律要求時除外。

7. 本機儲存與 Cookie

  • 我們不使用 Cookie。
  • 遊戲用瀏覽器的本機儲存（localStorage）存放：遊戲存檔與設定、語音偏好、匯入的題庫與教材版本、孩子角色的登入權杖，以及還沒同步到伺服器的存檔變更。家長與老師帳號的登入狀態預設存在 sessionStorage（關閉分頁就清除），勾選「在這台裝置保持登入」時才存在 localStorage。
  • 這些資料只存在你的裝置上，清除瀏覽器的網站資料就會刪除。
  • 使用 Google 登入按鈕時，Google 可能依其政策在 Google 自己的網域使用 Cookie。

8. 資料保存期限與刪除

  • 各項資料的保存期限見第 2 節的表格。
  • 單機遊玩的資料：清除瀏覽器的網站資料即可刪除。
  • 家長與老師的帳號：在帳號設定按「刪除帳號」並再輸入一次密碼（沒有密碼的帳號改用綁定的 Google 帳號確認是本人），就會刪除帳號、名下的雲端角色與進度、登入權杖、驗證與重設連結以及 Google 綁定；角色如果還在班級裡，還沒收下的禮物會先退回。還有班級的老師帳號要先處理班級（刪除會影響班上的孩子），請透過第 13 節的聯絡方式聯絡我們。
  • 家長名下的雲端角色：家長可以在帳號頁刪除個別角色，或讓角色退出班級（進度保留）。
  • 孩子在班級裡的資料：老師移出成員時，只會離開那一班（那一班的暱稱、密碼與用那一班代碼登入的權杖會刪除）；沒有家長帳號、也沒有其他班級的角色會刪除進度、收藏與登入權杖；家長名下的角色進度留在家長帳號。同學已經收到的禮物紀錄裡，仍會留著送禮人的暱稱。
  • Google 綁定：在帳號設定按「解除」即刪除。
  • 整個班級或其他資料：透過第 13 節的聯絡方式提出刪除要求；確認是該班的老師或孩子的家長後，我們會盡快刪除。

9. 兒童隱私

本遊戲的使用者是國小學童。孩子不需要註冊帳號：家長掃描老師給的 QR code、用家長帳號讓孩子的角色加入班級（不需要密碼）；在學校，老師在平板上輸入教室密碼後，從班上名單讓孩子進入，或建立沒有家長帳號的學生角色（之後老師可以給家長一張一次性的家長連結卡，家長登入後把孩子的角色連到自己的帳號，班上的進度都會留著）；孩子也可以用老師設定的「班級代碼＋暱稱＋4 位數密碼」登入。家長用自己的帳號管理孩子的角色。我們不收集孩子的真實姓名、生日、照片、聯絡方式或所在位置；電子郵件地址與 Google 帳號登入只給家長與老師使用；遊戲沒有可以自由打字的聊天。建議家長陪同孩子使用，並提醒孩子不要在暱稱裡使用真名。

10. 資料安全

  • 網站與班級伺服器的網址都使用 HTTPS 加密連線。
  • 家長與老師的密碼（至少 8 個字）、班級的教室密碼（至少 6 個字）與孩子的 4 位數密碼只保存無法還原的雜湊值（scrypt）；登入權杖、驗證與重設密碼的連結在伺服器上也只保存雜湊值。
  • 驗證連結 24 小時、重設密碼的連結 30 分鐘後失效，而且只能用一次；重設密碼後，這個帳號在所有裝置的登入都會登出。
  • 同一個帳號連續輸錯 5 次密碼會鎖住 5 分鐘，同一個班級的教室密碼也是；註冊、登入、加入班級、驗證電子郵件與重設密碼都有流量限制。申請重設密碼時，不論帳號是否存在都顯示同樣的訊息。
  • Google 登入憑證一律用 Google 公開的金鑰驗證。
  • 孩子的 4 位數密碼是方便孩子使用的措施，不是高強度的防護。教室密碼是全班共用的，只有老師知道；外流時老師更換，學校平板要重新輸入。

Learning Island (知識島大冒險) Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Learning Island (知識島大冒險) is a 3D learning game for second-grade elementary school children (https://kevintsai1202.github.io/learning-game/, mirror https://learning-island.zeabur.app/). This policy explains what data we collect, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, who can see it, and how data obtained through Google Sign-In is used. We only collect the data needed to provide the game. We show no ads, never sell or share data, and use no tracking or analytics tools.

1. Scope and operator

This policy covers the Learning Island website, game, and class server. The service is developed and operated by an individual developer (GitHub account kevintsai1202), referred to as "we". The game is free and can be played on a single device without registering. Parent and teacher accounts, joining a class, and Google Sign-In are optional. Accounts are for parents and teachers; children do not register: a parent scans the teacher's QR code to add the child to the class, or on a school tablet the teacher enters the classroom password to let the child in.

3. How we use data

  • Save and sync learning progress so children can continue on another device; parents can use their own account to save a child's character to the cloud and get it back on a new device.
  • Provide class features: a shared island, chat phrases, and gifts.
  • Let teachers manage their classes and see learning summaries.
  • Sign-in and account security: identify who is signed in and prevent password guessing and request floods.
  • Email addresses are used only to send verification and password-reset emails (see Section 6); we never send ads or newsletters.

We never use data for advertising, marketing, user analytics, or profiling, never sell it, and never use it to train AI models.

4. Google user data (Sign in with Google)

Parents and teachers can choose to register with their Google account, or link a Google account to their own account in account settings, and then sign in quickly with the Google button on the account page ("Continue with Google", shown in Chinese as 「透過 Google 帳戶繼續操作」). This is optional: an account registered with a username and password can always sign in with them; an account registered with Google needs no separate username or password, and a password can be added later in account settings. Children's characters are never linked to Google accounts.

  • Requested permissions: only openid, email, and profile (classified by Google as non-sensitive scopes). We never access Gmail mailboxes, Google Drive, contacts, calendars, or any other Google service data.
  • Data we receive: when you sign in, the Google-provided sign-in button (Google Identity Services) gives us an ID token signed by Google that contains the Google account ID, email address (and whether Google has verified it), name, and profile picture URL. The server verifies the token with Google's public keys.
  • How we use it: the Google account ID is used only to find the linked parent or teacher account, complete sign-in, and confirm it is you when you set a password or delete your account. When you register with Google, the email address verified by Google becomes your account's email address (used as described in Section 3), and the username is generated from the letters, digits, and underscores before the "@" in that address (only you can see it on the account page); the email address stored with a link is used only to show which Google account is linked in account settings, partially masked.
  • Data we store: only the Google account ID, email address, and link time, stored in the class server database. The name, profile picture, and any other token data are never stored.
  • Sharing and disclosure: Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, provided or transferred to any third party, or used to train AI models. No one views it except as needed to keep the system secure or as required by law. Teachers cannot see parents' account details or email addresses.
  • Deletion and revocation: you can choose "Unlink" in account settings at any time, and the server immediately deletes that link (Google account ID and email address; an account without a password must set one before unlinking its last Google account, so it can still sign in); deleting your account deletes all links. You can also remove Learning Island's access on your Google Account's "Third-party apps & services" page (https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
  • Limited Use disclosure: Learning Island's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who can see the data

  • Classmates in the same class: the nickname used in that class, character appearance, online status, which island the child is on (only "on the class island" or "on their own island", without saying which class; shown in the friends list), position on that class's island, and chat phrases (there is no free-text typing anywhere in the game); when choosing a gift, which outfits the recipient already has (to avoid duplicates); received gifts show the sender's nickname in that class. Classmates cannot see the nicknames or names of the child's other classes.
  • Siblings (cloud characters under the same parent account): in the friends list, nickname, character appearance, online status, and which island the child is on (only "on the class island" or "on their own island"; the class name is not shown), even if they are in different classes.
  • Teachers: for each child in their class, the nickname used in that class, stars, coins, number of mistakes, completed rounds (learning progress belongs to the whole character, including play on other class islands and their own island), when the child joined that class, last online time, and where the child is now (online status, this class's island, another class's island without saying which, or their own island, and which building). Teachers can reset a child's PIN for their class, remove the child from their class, and turn joining, chat, and gifts on or off. Teachers cannot see which other classes a child is in, or parents' account details or email addresses.
  • Parents: after signing in with a parent account, a summary of their own cloud characters (name, appearance, class, stars, coins, last online time); they can download a character to the current device, take it out of a class, or delete it. The parent area on a device shows the full progress and mistake notebook of the characters on that device.
  • Account owners: account settings show your own username, email address, roles, and linked Google accounts.
  • People outside a class cannot see any data in that class (siblings only see the friends-list data listed above).

6. Third-party services and data location

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): serves the website files. GitHub may log connection data (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Zeabur: used to deploy and manage the class server and database. The server and database are located in Taiwan; internet traffic is relayed through a gateway server in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Google (sign-in): Google's sign-in script is loaded only when a "Sign in with Google" button appears on screen; its use is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google (email): verification and password-reset emails are sent through Google's Gmail, so the recipient's email address and the message content (username and link) pass through Google's mail servers and are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Emails are sent from our Gmail account.
  • Otherwise we use no advertising, analytics, tracking, or other third-party services. We never provide data to third parties except as required by law.

7. Local storage and cookies

  • We do not use cookies.
  • The game uses the browser's localStorage for: game saves and settings, voice preferences, imported question banks and textbook editions, sign-in tokens for children's characters, and save changes not yet synced to the server. Parent and teacher sign-in is kept in sessionStorage by default (cleared when the tab is closed), and in localStorage only if "Stay signed in on this device" is checked.
  • This data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the browser's site data.
  • When you use the Google sign-in button, Google may use cookies on Google's own domains under its own policies.

8. Retention and deletion

  • See the table in Section 2 for each item's retention period.
  • Single-device data: delete it by clearing the browser's site data.
  • Parent and teacher accounts: choose "Delete account" in account settings and enter your password again (an account without a password confirms with its linked Google account instead) to delete the account, its cloud characters and their progress, sign-in tokens, verification and reset links, and Google links; if a character is still in a class, its unaccepted gifts are returned first. A teacher account that still has classes must deal with those classes first (deleting it would affect the children in them); please contact us as described in Section 13.
  • Parent-owned cloud characters: on the account page, parents can delete individual characters or take a character out of a class (its progress is kept).
  • A child's class membership: when a teacher removes a member, the child only leaves that class (the nickname, PIN, and sign-in tokens from that class's code are deleted); characters without a parent account and without other classes are deleted together with their progress, collections, and sign-in tokens; parent-owned characters keep their progress under the parent account. Gifts already received by classmates still show the sender's nickname.
  • Google links: choose "Unlink" in account settings.
  • Whole classes or other data: request deletion through the contact in Section 13; once we confirm you are the class teacher or the child's parent, we will delete it promptly.

9. Children's privacy

Our users are elementary school children. Children do not register accounts: a parent scans the teacher's QR code and adds the child's character to the class with the parent's own account (no PIN needed); at school, the teacher enters the classroom password on a tablet and lets the child in from the class roster, or creates a student character without a parent account (the teacher can later give the parent a one-time parent link card; after signing in, the parent links the child's character to their own account and the class progress is kept); a child can also sign in with the "class code + nickname + 4-digit PIN" that the teacher sets. Parents manage their children's characters with their own account. We do not collect children's real names, birthdays, photos, contact details, or location; email addresses and Google Sign-In are only for parents and teachers; there is no free-text chat in the game. We recommend that parents accompany their children and remind them not to use real names as nicknames.

10. Security

  • The website and class server addresses use HTTPS.
  • Parent and teacher passwords (at least 8 characters), classroom passwords (at least 6 characters), and children's 4-digit PINs are stored only as irreversible hashes (scrypt); sign-in tokens and the links in verification and password-reset emails are also stored only as hashes on the server.
  • Verification links expire after 24 hours and password-reset links after 30 minutes, and each can be used only once; resetting a password signs that account out on all devices.
  • Five wrong passwords in a row lock that account for 5 minutes, and the same applies to a class's classroom password; registering, signing in, joining a class, verifying an email, and resetting a password are rate-limited. A password-reset request always shows the same message whether or not the account exists.
  • Google ID tokens are always verified with Google's public keys.
  • Children's 4-digit PINs are a convenience for children, not strong protection. The classroom password is shared by the whole class and known only to the teacher; if it leaks, the teacher changes it and school tablets must enter it again.

In account settings you can view and change your email address, roles, and password, unlink Google accounts, or delete the whole account; learning progress can be viewed in the game, the parent area, and the account page. For other requests, use the contact in Section 13.
