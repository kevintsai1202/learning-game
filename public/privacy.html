知識島大冒險 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 3 日

English version below

「知識島大冒險」是給國小二年級孩子的 3D 學習遊戲（網址 https://kevintsai1202.github.io/learning-game/，備用網址 https://learning-island.zeabur.app/）。本政策說明我們收集哪些資料、為什麼收集、存在哪裡、保存多久、誰看得到，以及使用 Google 帳號登入時取得的資料如何使用。我們只收集提供遊戲功能需要的資料；不放廣告、不出售或分享資料，也不使用任何追蹤或分析工具。

1. 適用範圍與營運者

本政策適用於「知識島大冒險」的網站、遊戲與班級伺服器。本服務由個人開發者（GitHub 帳號 kevintsai1202）開發與維運，以下稱「我們」。遊戲免費，不需要註冊也可以單機遊玩；加入班級與 Google 帳號登入都是選用功能。

2. 我們收集的資料

資料 什麼時候收集 存放位置 保存多久
角色名字、角色外觀、學習進度（星星、金幣、錯題本、遊玩紀錄、獎章、收藏）與設定（單機遊玩） 開始遊玩之後 只存在這台裝置的瀏覽器，不傳送到伺服器 直到清除瀏覽器的網站資料

老師的房間：房間名稱、管理密碼（只存雜湊值）、班級功能的開關設定 老師建立房間時 班級伺服器的資料庫 房間存在期間

Google 帳號識別碼、電子郵件地址、綁定時間（見第 4 節） 家長或老師綁定 Google 帳號時 班級伺服器的資料庫 解除綁定、移出成員或刪除房間時刪除
連線的 IP 位址 建立房間、加入班級、登入時 伺服器的記憶體，不寫入資料庫 只用來限制短時間內的大量請求；伺服器重新啟動就清除

我們不收集孩子的真實姓名、生日、照片、聯絡方式或所在位置。遊戲不使用麥克風或相機，也不錄音；語音朗讀使用預錄的音檔或裝置內建的語音功能。

3. 資料的用途

  • 保存與同步學習進度，讓孩子換裝置也能接著玩。
  • 提供班級功能：多人同島、短句聊天、送禮物。
  • 讓老師管理班級、查看學習摘要。
  • 登入與帳號安全：辨識登入的人，防止猜密碼與大量請求。

我們不會把資料用於廣告、行銷、使用者分析或剖析，不會出售資料，也不會用來訓練人工智慧模型。

4. Google 使用者資料（使用 Google 帳號登入）

家長或老師可以選擇把 Google 帳號綁定到孩子的班級帳號或老師的房間，之後用 Google 帳號登入，不必再輸入代碼與密碼。這是選用功能，原本的代碼登入一直可以使用。

  • 要求的權限：只有 openid、email、profile（Google 歸類為非敏感範圍）。我們不會存取 Gmail、Google 雲端硬碟、聯絡人、日曆或任何其他 Google 服務的資料。
  • 取得的資料：登入時，Google 提供的登入按鈕（Google Identity Services）會交給我們一張由 Google 簽署的登入憑證（ID token），內含 Google 帳號識別碼、電子郵件地址、名稱與大頭貼網址。伺服器用 Google 公開的金鑰驗證憑證確實來自 Google。
  • 如何使用：Google 帳號識別碼只用來找出這個 Google 帳號綁定的班級帳號或房間，完成登入；電子郵件地址只用來在家長專區與老師管理頁顯示「綁定的是哪個帳號」，畫面上會遮住部分字元。
  • 保存的資料：只保存 Google 帳號識別碼、電子郵件地址與綁定時間，存在班級伺服器的資料庫。名稱、大頭貼與憑證裡的其他資料一律不保存。
  • 分享與揭露：Google 使用者資料不會出售、不會用於廣告、不會提供或轉移給任何第三方，也不會用來訓練人工智慧模型。除了維護系統安全或依法律要求之外，不會有人檢視這些資料。老師管理頁不會顯示家長綁定的電子郵件。
  • 刪除與撤銷：隨時可以在家長專區或老師管理頁按「解除綁定」，伺服器會立即刪除該筆紀錄（Google 帳號識別碼與電子郵件地址）。也可以到 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式和服務」頁面（https://myaccount.google.com/connections）移除「知識島大冒險」的登入權限。老師移出成員時，該帳號的 Google 綁定也會一併刪除。
  • 有限使用聲明：知識島大冒險對於從 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與轉移，將遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策（Google API Services User Data Policy），包括其中的有限使用（Limited Use）規定。

5. 誰看得到這些資料

  • 同一班的同學：暱稱、角色外觀、是否上線、在島上的位置、聊天短句（遊戲裡沒有可以自由打字的地方）；選禮物時看得到對方已經有哪些外觀（避免送重複）；收到的禮物會顯示送禮人的暱稱。
  • 老師：班上每個孩子的暱稱、星星、金幣、錯題數、完成回合數、加入時間、最後上線時間與是否上線。老師可以重設孩子的密碼、移出成員，以及開關加入、聊天與送禮功能。
  • 家長：用孩子的帳號登入後，看得到該帳號的完整進度與錯題本。
  • 班級以外的人看不到班級裡的任何資料。

6. 第三方服務與資料存放位置

  • GitHub Pages（GitHub, Inc.）：提供網站檔案。GitHub 可能依 GitHub 隱私權聲明記錄連線資料（例如 IP 位址）。
  • Zeabur：用來部署與管理班級伺服器和資料庫。伺服器與資料庫位於台灣；網際網路連線經由位於日本東京的閘道伺服器轉送。
  • Google：只有畫面出現「使用 Google 帳戶登入」按鈕時，才會載入 Google 的登入程式；使用時適用 Google 隱私權政策。
  • 除此之外，不使用廣告、分析、追蹤或其他第三方服務。我們不會把資料提供給第三方，法律要求時除外。

7. 本機儲存與 Cookie

  • 我們不使用 Cookie。
  • 遊戲用瀏覽器的本機儲存（localStorage）存放：遊戲存檔與設定、語音偏好、匯入的題庫與教材版本、班級登入資訊，以及還沒同步到伺服器的存檔變更。老師管理頁的登入狀態存在 sessionStorage，關閉分頁就清除。
  • 這些資料只存在你的裝置上，清除瀏覽器的網站資料就會刪除。
  • 使用 Google 登入按鈕時，Google 可能依其政策在 Google 自己的網域使用 Cookie。

8. 資料保存期限與刪除

  • 各項資料的保存期限見第 2 節的表格。
  • 單機遊玩的資料：清除瀏覽器的網站資料即可刪除。
  • 孩子的班級帳號：老師在管理頁移出成員時，伺服器會刪除該帳號的進度、收藏、登入權杖與 Google 綁定；同學已經收到的禮物紀錄裡，仍會留著送禮人的暱稱。
  • Google 綁定：家長專區或老師管理頁按「解除綁定」即刪除。
  • 整個班級或其他資料：家長或老師可以透過第 13 節的聯絡方式提出刪除要求；確認是該班的老師或孩子的家長後，我們會盡快刪除。

9. 兒童隱私

本遊戲的使用者是國小學童。孩子只用「房間代碼＋暱稱＋4 位數密碼」加入老師建立的班級，我們不收集孩子的真實姓名、生日、照片、聯絡方式或所在位置；遊戲沒有可以自由打字的聊天。Google 帳號登入是給家長與老師使用的選項。建議家長陪同孩子使用，並提醒孩子不要在暱稱裡使用真名。

10. 資料安全

  • 網站與班級伺服器的網址都使用 HTTPS 加密連線。
  • 管理密碼與孩子的 4 位數密碼只保存無法還原的雜湊值（scrypt）；登入權杖在伺服器上也只保存雜湊值。
  • 同一個帳號連續輸錯 5 次密碼會鎖住 5 分鐘；建立房間、加入班級與登入都有流量限制。
  • Google 登入憑證一律用 Google 公開的金鑰驗證。
  • 孩子的 4 位數密碼是方便孩子使用的措施，不是高強度的防護。

11. 你的權利

依中華民國《個人資料保護法》，你可以請求查詢或閱覽、製給複製本、補充或更正、停止蒐集處理或利用，以及刪除你的個人資料。學習進度可以在遊戲與家長專區查看；Google 綁定可以隨時解除；其他請求請透過第 13 節的聯絡方式提出。

12. 政策更新

政策有變動時，會更新本頁內容與最上方的日期。

13. 聯絡我們

在 GitHub 專案頁提出問題：https://github.com/kevintsai1202/learning-game/issues。這是公開頁面，請只留下房間代碼與暱稱，不要留下孩子的真實姓名或其他個人資料。

Learning Island (知識島大冒險) Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Learning Island (知識島大冒險) is a 3D learning game for second-grade elementary school children (https://kevintsai1202.github.io/learning-game/, mirror https://learning-island.zeabur.app/). This policy explains what data we collect, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, who can see it, and how data obtained through Google Sign-In is used. We only collect the data needed to provide the game. We show no ads, never sell or share data, and use no tracking or analytics tools.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the Learning Island website, game, and class server. The service is developed and operated by an individual developer (GitHub account kevintsai1202), referred to as "we" below. The game is free and can be played on a single device without registering; joining a class and signing in with Google are optional.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect children's real names, birthdays, photos, contact details, or location. The game does not use the microphone or camera and records no audio; read-aloud uses pre-recorded audio files or the device's built-in speech feature.

3. How we use data

  • To save and sync learning progress so a child can continue on another device.
  • To provide class features: playing on the same island, phrase chat, and gifts.
  • To let teachers manage their class and view learning summaries.
  • For sign-in and account security: identifying who signs in and preventing password guessing and request floods.

We do not use data for advertising, marketing, user analytics, or profiling. We do not sell data, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Google user data (Sign in with Google)

Parents or teachers may optionally link a Google account to a child's class account or to a teacher's room, and then sign in with Google instead of entering codes and passwords. This is optional; code-based sign-in always remains available.

  • Scopes requested: only openid, email, and profile (classified by Google as non-sensitive). We do not access Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google service data.
  • Data accessed: at sign-in, the Google Identity Services button gives us an ID token signed by Google that contains the Google account ID, email address, name, and profile picture URL. Our server verifies the token with Google's public keys.
  • How it is used: the Google account ID is used only to find the class account or room linked to that Google account and complete sign-in. The email address is used only to show which account is linked in the parent area and the teacher admin page, with part of it masked.
  • What is stored: only the Google account ID, email address, and link time, in the class server database. The name, profile picture, and any other token contents are never stored.
  • Sharing and disclosure: Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with or transferred to any third party, and never used to train artificial intelligence models. No one reads this data except as needed for security or to comply with the law. Teachers cannot see the email addresses linked by parents.
  • Deletion and revocation: you can press "Unlink" in the parent area or the teacher admin page at any time, and the server immediately deletes that record (Google account ID and email address). You can also remove Learning Island's sign-in access on the "Third-party apps & services" page of your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com/connections). When a teacher removes a member, that account's Google links are deleted as well.
  • Limited Use disclosure: Learning Island's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who can see the data

  • Classmates in the same class: nickname, character appearance, online status, position on the island, and chat phrases (there is no free-text input anywhere in the game); when choosing a gift, which appearance items the recipient already owns (to avoid duplicates); and the sender's nickname on received gifts.
  • The teacher: each child's nickname, stars, coins, number of wrong answers, completed rounds, join time, last-seen time, and online status. The teacher can reset a child's PIN, remove members, and turn joining, chat, and gifts on or off.
  • Parents: after signing in with the child's account, the account's full progress and wrong-answer notebook.
  • No one outside the class can see any class data.

6. Third-party services and data location

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): serves the website files. GitHub may log connection data (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Zeabur: used to deploy and manage the class server and database. The server and database are located in Taiwan; internet connections are relayed through a gateway server in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Google: Google's sign-in script is loaded only when a "Sign in with Google" button is shown; its use is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • We use no other advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party services, and we do not provide data to third parties except when required by law.

7. Local storage and cookies

  • We do not use cookies.
  • The game uses the browser's localStorage for save data and settings, voice preferences, imported question packs and textbook editions, class sign-in information, and progress changes not yet synced to the server. The teacher admin page keeps its sign-in state in sessionStorage, which is cleared when the tab is closed.
  • This data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the browser's site data.
  • When the Google sign-in button is used, Google may use cookies on its own domains under its policies.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Retention periods for each kind of data are listed in the table in Section 2.
  • Single-device data: delete it by clearing the browser's site data.
  • A child's class account: when the teacher removes the member on the admin page, the server deletes that account's progress, collections, sign-in tokens, and Google links. Gift records already received by classmates keep the sender's nickname.
  • Google links: deleted by pressing "Unlink" in the parent area or the teacher admin page.
  • A whole class or other data: parents or teachers can request deletion through the contact method in Section 13. After confirming the requester is the class's teacher or the child's parent, we will delete it as soon as possible.

9. Children's privacy

The game is used by elementary school children. Children join a teacher-created class with only a room code, a nickname, and a 4-digit PIN. We do not collect children's real names, birthdays, photos, contact details, or location, and there is no free-text chat. Google Sign-In is an option for parents and teachers. We recommend that parents accompany their children and remind them not to use their real names as nicknames.

10. Security

  • The website and the class server use HTTPS (encrypted) connections.
  • Admin passwords and children's 4-digit PINs are stored only as irreversible hashes (scrypt); sign-in tokens are also stored only as hashes on the server.
  • Five consecutive wrong passwords lock the account for 5 minutes; creating rooms, joining classes, and signing in are rate-limited.
  • Google ID tokens are always verified with Google's public keys.
  • A child's 4-digit PIN is a convenience for children, not strong protection.

11. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you may request to inquire about or review, obtain copies of, supplement or correct, stop the collection, processing, or use of, and delete your personal data. Learning progress can be viewed in the game and the parent area, Google links can be removed at any time, and other requests can be made through the contact method in Section 13.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

13. Contact us

Open an issue on the GitHub project page: https://github.com/kevintsai1202/learning-game/issues. This page is public, so please leave only the room code and nickname, never a child's real name or other personal data.
